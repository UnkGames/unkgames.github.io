Goal BOO

About the Game

Welcome to Goal BOO, a lighthearted and funny competitive game created by Unknown Games Studio. Step into the arena for an action-packed two-player showdown where the goal is simple: smash, blast, and destroy your opponent's target boxes before they wipe out yours!

Proudly carrying the spirit of Iraqi culture, every match brings lively local vibes, playful surprises, and unpredictable moments that will keep you and your rival laughing until the final box falls.

1v1 Box Mayhem

Go head-to-head against a rival player. Defend your turf while launching chaotic attacks to shatter their goal boxes.

Pure Comedy

Expect funny reactions, wild physics, and hilarious comebacks in fast-paced matches anyone can jump into.

Iraqi Culture & Flavor

Celebrate Iraqi heritage through vibrant themes, local humor, and energetic style woven right into the arena.
