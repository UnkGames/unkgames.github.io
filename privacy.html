Privacy Policy

Game: Goal BOO  |  Developer: Unknown Games Studio  |  Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Unknown Games Studio ("we," "our," or "us") built Goal BOO as a commercial/free-to-play game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you play our game on mobile or desktop platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We strive to collect as little personal data as possible. Depending on how you play Goal BOO, the following technical and gameplay data may be processed:

  • Account & Multiplayer Identifiers: Anonymous Device IDs, Product User IDs (PUID), or platform account IDs (such as Google Play Games or Epic Games Account IDs) required to connect you to online matches.
  • Gameplay & Cloud Save Data: Match progress, unlocked items, game settings, and session statistics stored locally or in cloud storage.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Basic technical details such as device model, operating system version, crash logs, and IP address (used strictly for network routing and anti-cheat security).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to:

  • Operate online multiplayer matchmaking, sessions, and lobbies.
  • Save and restore your game progress across sessions.
  • Monitor performance, fix bugs, and prevent cheating or abuse.

3. Third-Party Services

Goal BOO uses trusted third-party backend services to power online features and distribution. These providers may process technical identifiers according to their own privacy policies:

  • Epic Online Services (EOS): Used for multiplayer matchmaking, session management, and cloud saves (Epic Games Privacy Policy).
  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, platform authentication, and crash reporting on Android (Google Privacy Policy).

4. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. All online data transmissions between your device and our backend services are encrypted using industry-standard security protocols.

5. Children's Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
