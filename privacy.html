Privacy Policy

Game: Goal BOO  |  Developer: Unknown Games Studio  |  Effective Date: November 11, 2026

Unknown Games Studio ("we," "our," or "us") built Goal BOO as a commercial/free-to-play game. This Privacy Policy explains how personal and technical information is collected, used, shared, retained, and protected when you play our game on mobile or desktop platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We strive to collect as little personal data as possible. Depending on how you play Goal BOO, the following technical and gameplay information may be collected and processed:

  • Account & Multiplayer Identifiers: Anonymous Device IDs, Product User IDs (PUID), or platform account identifiers (such as Epic Games Account IDs or Google Play Games IDs) required to authenticate your session and connect you to online matches.
  • Gameplay & Cloud Save Data: Match progress, unlocked items, game settings, and session statistics stored locally on your device or in cloud storage.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Basic technical details such as device model, operating system version, crash logs, and IP address (used strictly for network routing, stability, and anti-cheat security).

2. How We Use Your Information

We handle and use the collected information strictly to:

  • Operate online multiplayer matchmaking, sessions, and lobbies.
  • Save and restore your game progress across sessions.
  • Monitor performance, diagnose technical issues, and prevent cheating or abuse.
  • Respond to player support and data protection inquiries.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share necessary technical identifiers and gameplay data with trusted infrastructure and platform providers solely to operate online features and distribute Goal BOO.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy relationships with you and govern their own data practices through their respective privacy policies:

  • Epic Games: Integrated for platform authentication and online game services (Epic Games Privacy Policy).
  • Google Play Services: Integrated for Android distribution, platform authentication, and crash diagnostics (Google Privacy Policy).

4. Data Retention & Security

We retain your gameplay data and account identifiers only for as long as necessary to provide online services for Goal BOO, maintain your saved progress, or comply with applicable legal obligations. Diagnostic and crash logs are automatically deleted after 90 days. If your account remains inactive for an extended period or you request deletion, your associated online records are permanently erased from our systems.

All online data transmissions between your device and our backend services are encrypted using industry-standard security protocols.

5. Children's Privacy

Our game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

6. Your Rights, Data Access & Deletion

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal information, online gameplay data, and account identifiers. To submit a data access or deletion request, or for any data protection inquiries, please contact our Data Protection Contact at:

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new game features or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
